Resumight is a resume building tool for students and new job seekers to get their accomplishments seen. Make a resume, tailor it to every job, and get coaching to nail the interview.

**Demo Link (use Chromium browser for best experience):** https://resumight.ai.studio/

Resumight comes equipped with three main tools: Resumaker, Tailor, and Coach. These are what I thought would be most useful to job larpers like me (who are not AI shipwrights please use AI checker im begging you).

# Resumaker
Resumaker is what helps you get your first resume into a PDF format. Simply add in achievements, awards, education, and more. What's really great about Resumaker is that it even lets you use your voice to talk, but not in a strict way. You can just speak normally, as if you were describing your past to someone you just met. Resumaker will sort everything into the category where it belongs.
E.g. "I studied comp eng at waterloo" --> Adds Bachelor of Computer Engineering (University of Waterloo) to Education section

# Tailor
Tailor is what makes your resume stand out from the crowd, letting you make the most out of your qualifications without having to spend valuable time poring over hundreds of job descriptions. Once you have a resume, either one from resumaker or your own, you're ready to tailor it. Simply paste in the entire job description. Tailor can do more than find matching words - using Gemini AI, it can actively cut things from your resume that might be negative for the job at hand, while emphasising the most valuable parts.

# Coach
Coach is what helps translate your skills on paper into something you can actually talk about. Inputting your resume and the role you're targeting allows the AI to create notes and even short scripts that help you present yourself in the best way possible. This is one of the most crucial steps for those applying to entry-level jobs, since many first-time job seekers freeze up when asked questions on the spot. 

# Extra
Resumight's AI integration is done right: Actual smart usage as opposed to strictly fishing for keywords. This is because the AI that analyses has been specifically prompted to give the kinds of responses that would be useful to actual Resumight users.
